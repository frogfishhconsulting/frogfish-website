Why AEO and paid media belong together

  • They hit the same buyer, minutes apart. A prospect asks ChatGPT "best personal injury lawyer near me", sees three names, then searches one on Google and clicks an ad. If AEO and paid are run separately, neither sees the full path, and each claims the conversion.

  • AEO lowers paid costs. Buyers who have already seen your name in an AI answer search for you by name, click more and convert better, so brand demand rises and paid gets cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

Can one agency really do AEO and paid media well?

Yes, if both are run off the same data. The skills overlap more than most people think: both depend on understanding buyer questions, intent and conversion data. What usually fails is splitting them across agencies that measure success differently.

What is AEO?

Answer engine optimization (also called GEO or AI search optimization) is the work of getting your business named and cited in AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. It combines answer-first content, structured data and third-party proof.

Do I need AEO if paid media is working?

Paid media rents attention. AEO earns a recommendation at the moment buyers are deciding, and it makes paid cheaper by raising brand searches. Most lead-gen businesses should run both, with paid funding growth now and AEO compounding over 6 to 12 months.

How do you measure AI search results?

Share of AI answers that mention you across a fixed question set, AI referral traffic, and, most importantly, consults, signed customers and revenue from AI-referred leads in your CRM.

Who is FrogFish best for?

Lead-gen and service businesses spending $10K or more a month on paid media, with sales that close offline: law firms, solar and home services, clinical trials and healthcare, real estate and lending.
