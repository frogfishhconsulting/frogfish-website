Why legal PPC gets expensive

Legal keywords are some of the most expensive in Google Ads, and most of the clicks never become cases: wrong practice area, wrong state, existing clients, solicitors, spam. When a campaign is optimized to calls or form fills, Google keeps buying more of those. The intake team drowns and cost per signed case climbs.

How we run law firm PPC

  1. Track to the signed case. We connect your intake system or CRM (Clio, Lead Docket, Filevine, Salesforce and others) and send qualified, consult and signed stages back to Google, Microsoft and Meta. See offline conversion tracking.
  2. Clean the inputs. Spam and solicitor filtering, call qualification, negative keywords and placement exclusions, so the algorithm stops learning from junk.
  3. Rebuild around case value. Practice areas, geos and keywords are judged on cost per signed case and case value. Budget moves to what signs.
  4. Fix speed to lead. Leads called back within minutes sign far more often. We set up AI intake and follow-up agents and Slack alerts when leads sit uncalled.
