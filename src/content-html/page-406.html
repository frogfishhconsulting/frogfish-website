How we operate

Five layers. One operating system.

Whether you walk in through Door 01 or Door 02, the engagement runs on the same five layers. We diagnose which layer needs the most work first.

01 — DISCOVERY

Paid, AEO & SEO

Every prospective client takes a different path in — a Google search, a paid ad, a question to ChatGPT or Perplexity. We make sure your firm shows up across all of them, on the high-intent queries that actually convert to consultations.

02 — INTELLIGENCE

The data layer

We tie your case management system (Filevine, Clio, Litify, MyCase, etc.) to your campaigns. No more guessing which ad drove the call that signed the case six weeks later.

03 — TARGETING

The platform layer

We feed signed-case signal into ad platforms so they optimize for revenue, not form fills. Paired with lookalike audiences built on your actual client roster.

04 — INTAKE

The conversion layer

We score leads as they come in, route the qualified ones to your intake team instantly, and filter the noise — so your staff isn’t burning hours on prospects who’ll never sign.

05 — NURTURE

The CRM layer

You’re sitting on a landmine of data — past clients, unsigned leads, referrals, prior consultations. Most firms never touch it again. We build the nurture campaigns and re-engagement flows that turn that dormant list into signed cases without spending another dollar on ads.
