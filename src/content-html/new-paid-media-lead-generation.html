Why most lead-gen accounts plateau

Ad platforms optimize to whatever conversion you give them. For most lead-gen businesses that is a form fill or a call. The algorithm gets very good at finding cheap form fills, and cheap form fills are often the people least likely to buy: spam, tire-kickers, people outside your service area, people who never answer the phone. Cost per lead looks great while cost per customer quietly climbs.

The fix isn't a new campaign structure. It is telling the platforms which leads actually turned into revenue, and letting the bidding chase those.

How we run paid media for lead-gen businesses

  1. Connect the CRM to the ad accounts. We send your downstream stages (qualified, consult booked, signed, funded) back to Google, Microsoft and Meta as offline conversions, usually nightly.
  2. Score leads early. When the real outcome takes weeks, we build a lead score from the first-party data you already collect, so the platforms get a strong signal within hours, not months.
  3. Rebuild the accounts around that signal. Search terms, geos, audiences and creative are judged on cost per signed customer. Spend moves to where revenue comes from, not where clicks are cheap.
  4. Watch it every day. Alerts go to your team's Slack when a market stops converting, spam spikes or a channel has headroom, so changes happen in days, not at the monthly review.

Frequently asked questions

What is a lead generation paid media agency?

An agency that runs paid advertising (Google, Microsoft, Meta, LinkedIn) for businesses that sell through leads rather than online checkout. The good ones optimize to closed revenue in the CRM, not to cost per lead.

Why optimize to signed customers instead of cost per lead?

Because the ad platforms find whatever you reward. Rewarding form fills buys cheap, low-quality leads. Rewarding signed customers (via offline conversions or lead scores) teaches the bidding to find buyers, which lowers cost per customer even when cost per lead goes up.

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising, Meta, LinkedIn, Reddit and connected TV. Most lead-gen clients get the biggest gains from Google and Microsoft search plus Meta, tied together with offline conversion data.

How much do I need to spend?

We work best with businesses spending $10K or more a month on paid media. Below that, there usually isn't enough conversion data for the platforms to learn from downstream outcomes.

How fast do results show up?

Tracking and offline conversions are usually live in 2 to 4 weeks. Bidding typically needs another 4 to 8 weeks of data before cost per customer moves meaningfully.
