Why we're different

Most agencies stop at the lead. We follow it to the contract.

A form fill isn't revenue. Your real outcome happens offline, as a booked consult, a signed case, a funded loan or a closed sale. So that's what we track, and what we feed back into Google, Meta and Microsoft to optimize against.

A typical agency

  • Optimizes to cost per lead
  • Ad platforms only see the form fill
  • Monthly PDF report, weeks after the fact
  • Leads go cold after one automated email
  • A junior account manager runs your account

FrogFish

  • Optimizes to cost per booked consult, signed case or funded loan
  • Your CRM outcomes flow back to the ad platforms as offline conversions
  • A live pipeline dashboard and daily Slack alerts
  • Lead scoring and nurture flows that work every lead
  • Ex-Google operators, with AI doing the daily work
Offline informs online

It's a loop, not a funnel.

A funnel ends at the sale. Ours doesn't stop there. What happens offline, in your phone calls, consults and contracts, flows back into how we bid, target and create. Every turn of the loop makes the next one cheaper.

  1. Ad platforms Google, Microsoft, Meta
  2. Lead comes in Click, call or form
  3. Your team works it Speed to lead and nurture
  4. Offline outcome Consult, case, loan, sale
  5. FrogFish AI learns Value, quality, market, hook
  6. Smarter targeting Bids, audiences, budgets, ads

What your offline data teaches the ad platforms

Bidding

Booked consults and signed deals, with their dollar values, go back as conversions. Google and Microsoft learn to chase people who close, not people who fill out forms.

Audiences

Your best customers seed lookalike audiences. Junk leads and current customers get excluded.

Budgets by market

Spend moves to the metros where leads turn into consults, and away from the ones where they don't.

Keywords

Search terms that bring leads who never book get cut, even when their cost per lead looks cheap.

Creative

What closers say on sales calls becomes the hook for next week's ads.

Your sales team

Slow follow-up and no-shows get flagged, because the best ad can't fix a lead nobody called.
