FAQ

Questions lead-gen businesses ask us.

What does offline conversion tracking mean for a lead-gen business?

We take what happens after the lead, such as a sit, a signature or a funded loan, from your CRM and send it back to Google, Meta and Microsoft. The platforms then bid for people who close, not just people who fill out forms.

Which CRMs do you work with?

Salesforce, HubSpot and GoHighLevel most often, plus call tracking tools like CallRail. If your outcomes live in another system, we connect that too.

How much do we need to spend on ads?

We work best with businesses spending $10K or more a month on paid media, with a sales process that closes offline.

Do you replace our sales team's tools?

No. We plug into the CRM and phone system you already use, so there's nothing to rip out.
