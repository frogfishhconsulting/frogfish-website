FAQ

AI agents, answered.

What is an agentic workflow?

It's a set of AI agents that each own one job, like intake, qualification, scheduling, documents or reporting. They're connected to your tools and to each other, so work moves from step to step without someone pushing it. People step in only where you want them to.

What can AI agents do for a law firm?

They answer and qualify every lead 24/7, run conflict checks, book consults, chase retainers and medical records, open matters in your case management system, keep clients updated, and send signed cases back to your ad platforms, so spend moves toward cases that sign.

How do you decide which agents to build?

We start by learning your business: how a lead becomes revenue, who touches it and where it stalls. Then we build the agents that remove the biggest bottlenecks first and add more from there.

Do the agents replace my staff?

No. They take over the repetitive work, like chasing, data entry and after-hours coverage, so your team spends its time on clients and decisions. Humans approve anything sensitive.

Which systems do you connect to?

Your CRM or case management system (Salesforce, HubSpot, Filevine, Clio, Litify and others), phones and text, calendars, e-signature, Slack, and the Google, Meta and Microsoft ad platforms.
