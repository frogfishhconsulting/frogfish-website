An Ex-Google Performance Marketing Agency

FrogFish is a performance marketing agency founded and led by Alex Fleshner, who spent 13 years at Google, including leading the Google Ads lead-gen vertical, serving as Head of Media Strategy at Verily and as AI Transformation Lead at Google Cloud AI. That background shapes how we work: we build accounts the way Google's own lead-gen specialists advise the largest advertisers, with first-party data and offline conversions at the center.

What 13 years at Google changes

  • We know how the bidding learns. Smart Bidding and Performance Max are only as good as the conversion signal. Most accounts feed them form fills. We feed them signed customers and lead scores.
  • We've seen what top lead-gen advertisers do. Leading the Google Ads lead-gen vertical meant working with the largest lead-gen advertisers on measurement, offline conversions and value-based bidding. We bring that playbook to businesses spending $10K+ a month.
  • AI is built in, not bolted on. From Google Cloud AI we bring practical AI: lead scoring models, AI agents for intake and reporting, and AI search optimization so you're named in ChatGPT and AI Overviews.
  • Regulated categories are familiar. At Verily we ran media strategy in healthcare, where privacy and measurement both matter. We apply the same care to clinical trials, legal and lending.

What you get

Senior people on every account, no junior hand-off. One team for paid media, AI search, attribution and lead scoring and AI agents, measured on cost per customer in your CRM.
