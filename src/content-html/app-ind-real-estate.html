The problem

Real estate marketing is measured on the wrong things.

Portals count leads. Ad platforms count clicks. Neither counts signed contracts, executed leases or closed sales. That's the gap we close.

01

The click isn't the sale.

A buyer clicks an ad, tours weeks later and closes 60 days after that. Most attribution stops at the click.

02

Lead quality is a black hole.

Zillow, StreetEasy, Google Ads and Instagram DMs all land in one pile. Agents burn time on browsers while real buyers go cold.

03

Your CRM doesn't talk to your ads.

Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Salesforce and Yardi hold the deal data. Google and Meta never see it.

FAQ

Questions real estate teams ask us.

Can you track a real estate lead from ad click to closing?

Yes. We connect your CRM (Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, Salesforce, Yardi, VTS and others) to Google and Meta, so showings, executed leases and closings are matched back to the ad and sent to the platforms as conversions.

What does the AI concierge do?

It replies to every lead in seconds by text, chat or DM, answers questions using your listings, qualifies the buyer, books showings on agents' calendars, updates the CRM and alerts the agent in Slack. A human can take over any conversation.

Is the concierge fair-housing compliant?

It's built with fair housing guardrails: it answers about properties, not about who lives in a neighborhood, and it hands sensitive questions to a human.

Do you work with commercial real estate?

Yes. For CRE we focus on long-cycle attribution, LinkedIn firmographic targeting and LoopNet, Crexi and CoStar retargeting.
